# S2.1 — Practical Position Gate

Status: **DIAGNOSTIC NEGATIVE SIGNAL — NO ELO DECISION**

This gate compares the approved `Current` profile with the isolated
`current-threat-aware` candidate on saved positions from the stopped D1.14
pilot. It does not modify `Current`, start a match, or convert local position
results into Elo.

## Scope and provenance

The position manifest is
`tests/data/s2.1-practical-positions.json` and contains nine positions:

```text
king-danger          3
defensive-resource   3
control              3
```

The labels are analyst labels for a practical diagnostic. They are not mate
oracles, objective defence proofs, or a strength test. The positions were
selected from the committed D1.14 Stockfish postmortem JSONL files:


The saved Stockfish move in the manifest is retained only as a diagnostic
reference. It was generated by the earlier D1.14 postmortem at different
node budgets and is not an independent oracle for this gate.

## Execution contract

Each row used a fresh release process and a cold 16 MB TT:

```text
profiles:       current vs current-threat-aware
Hash:           16 MB
threads:        1
TT mode:        cold, one process per profile/position/time
time limits:    1,000 ms / 3,000 ms / 10,000 ms
positions:      9
searches:       3 × 9 × 2 = 54
```

The runner first performed a UCI identity probe for each executable, then used
the profile bench entry with the exact FEN. It recorded score-by-depth,
completed depth, bestmove, PV, nodes, elapsed time, NPS, and:

```text
check_extensions
single_evasion_extensions
qsearch_check_moves
```

The release binary was built from `b6e784d0c4332b3e63e0596bc29cbe41d6e0b572`.
The two executable copies had the same SHA-256, as expected; their only
intended difference was the selected profile:

```text
7df6fc360989afc35a51cea7402f6bec573f31362a6a8dacc407f0c6a68712d7
```

Before building the candidate, the TT layout was measured at both the
pre-context baseline `0abd984` and the fixed tip `aaece03`:

```text
                         0abd984   aaece03
TtKey                    24        24 bytes
TTEntry                  40        40 bytes
Option<TTEntry>          40        40 bytes
Hash 16 capacity         419430    419430 entries
```

The forcing-budget context therefore did not reduce the configured 16 MB
entry density.

The complete row-level artifact is
`results/s2.1/practical-gate.json`.

## Depth result

The final artifact completed all 54 searches without a process, protocol, or
stderr failure. Candidate depth uplift means the candidate completed a higher
depth than `Current` for the same position and time limit.

| time | Current median depth | candidate median depth | candidate uplift | Current uplift | equal depth |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 s  | 5 | 3 | 0/9 | 8/9 | 1/9 |
| 3 s  | 5 | 4 | 0/9 | 9/9 | 0/9 |
| 10 s | 6 | 4 | 0/9 | 9/9 | 0/9 |

This is a negative fixed-time depth signal for the candidate on this
practical slice. The candidate does execute its intended extra work, but the
bounded extensions and qsearch checks do not compensate for their cost here.
The result is not an Elo estimate and is not a proof that the candidate is
weaker in every position.

`Current` reported zero for these candidate-only counters. The candidate's
bestmove matched the saved Stockfish reference on 5/9, 6/9 and 7/9 positions
at 1 s, 3 s and 10 s respectively; `Current` matched 3/9, 4/9 and 5/9. This
does not override the depth result: the reference moves came from earlier
postmortem searches, and the comparison is not an independent teacher gate.
