## Predefined gate decision

The candidate did not fail because of process integrity: all 63 Stockfish
searches completed, all 150 mappings were produced, and the source hashes
were checked before search. It failed the declared teacher-quality criteria.

The raw negative CPL anomalies are reported separately because fixed-node
unrestricted and forced searches can differ slightly under search noise. They
were not hidden by truncating the values to zero and were not used to reopen
the candidate after the hard mate/group failures.
