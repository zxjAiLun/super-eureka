## Fixed roles and provenance


`tests/data/openings/d1.14-openings-v1.epd`: 4,852 positions selected from

## CP-only Stockfish postmortem

The stopped pilot was analyzed diagnostically with the stable official
Stockfish 18 Windows AVX2 binary (release tag `sf_18`, archive SHA-256
`6f6c272ebd6ea594377715235c8a7326f75940ef4f4f856f45106028fe6ae900`; the
extracted executable hash is recorded in the JSON artifact). The analyzer uses
one Stockfish search for the unrestricted best move and one `searchmoves`
search for the played move at the same node limit. It records ordinary
centipawn loss and explicit mate outcomes separately; `UCI_ShowWDL` is not
requested or used.

The canonical screen used 20,000 nodes per search over 1,000 representative
positions drawn from the 826 complete games. The deeper review used 500,000
nodes per search on the 150 highest-risk screen positions. These are selected
diagnostic samples, not an objective Elo oracle and not a replacement for an
independent match.

The screen was mixed and small in effect:

```text
                         Current       CurrentLmr
mean / median CPL       60.47 / 22    59.72 / 22
blunders                 43            42
harmful mate swings     26            21
best-move match         38.74%        39.68%
```

On the deeper selected-risk set, mean CPL was `270.42` for Current and
`267.83` for CurrentLmr; harmful mate swings were `20` and `12`. However, the
sample is intentionally enriched for losses, and only 41 common-position pairs
had comparable CP scores: candidate lower CPL `0`, baseline lower CPL `1`,
equal `40`. This is mixed/inconclusive evidence, not a positive strength signal.

Canonical artifacts are:

```text
results/d1.14/postmortem-screen-20k-v3/
results/d1.14/postmortem-deep-500k/
```

The postmortem decision is therefore:

```text

CurrentLmr:         CANDIDATE-ONLY
```
