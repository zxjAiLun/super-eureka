## Correctness and isolation

The Rust and process-level tests verify that:

- `Current` has zero LMR reductions and re-searches;
- `current-lmr` executes real reductions and full-depth re-searches;
- first/PV moves, shallow nodes, in-check nodes, captures, promotions,
  en-passant, checking quiet moves, and low-material positions are not reduced;
- a reduced search that improves alpha is re-searched at the original depth;
- the re-searched PV and score are committed, and root FEN/Zobrist state is
  restored;
- `current-lmr` has no aspiration, null, futility, or qsearch SEE pruning
  counters enabled;
- the D1.10 project corpus at depth 7 completes `46/46` searches;
- the D1.11 external source slice at depth 6 completes `64/64` searches.

The deep corpus runs are differential safety checks, not mate or strength
oracles. They do not require the two profiles to produce identical PVs.
